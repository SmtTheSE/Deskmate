# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

First public release.

### Added
- Browser setup wizard with School, University and Company templates, a Test connection button for every service, and drag-and-drop documents.
- Inbox and approval queue: AI drafts with sources wait for a person to approve, edit or discard them.
- Pipeline: personal details hidden from the AI, safety screen that sends sensitive messages to a person, triage by department and urgency, and replies drafted only from your documents.
- AI choices: built-in offline assistant, OpenAI, Anthropic, and local models through Ollama.
- Channels: public help form with a private status page, email replies, an inbound email webhook, and Twilio SMS and WhatsApp.
- Optional automatic replies per department, for confident answers that come from your documents.
- Activity log, team accounts with temporary passwords, password changes and resets, and backup download.
- Install options: desktop app (Windows, Mac, Linux), one-click Render deploy, Railway, Fly.io, and Docker Compose with a one-command installer.
